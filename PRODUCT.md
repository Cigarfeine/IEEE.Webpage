## Stack
Static HTML5, CSS3 Custom Properties Token Architecture, ES6 Vanilla JS, Lenis Inertial Smooth Scroll, GSAP ScrollTrigger, React Bits Canvas Engines, zero-build deployment.

## Users
- Engineering students across Kerala and Region 10 competing in flagship statewide hackathons (WebNova '26, Smart India Hackathon, Tin-Her-Hack).
- Enrolled MBITS student engineers seeking hands-on systems tracks, silicon hardware labs, competitive algorithms, and active chapter membership.
- Academic faculty, IEEE distinguished keynote speakers, and computing researchers evaluating chapter milestones and publications.

## Product Purpose
Deliver an avant-garde digital home and operations nexus for IEEE Computer Society SBC-61241 (MBITS). The portal facilitates competition registration, research access, event schedule discovery, archive exploration, and student membership onboarding. Success means high conversion for hackathons and memberships, effortless technical credibility, and an uncompromising editorial craft.

## Positioning
Research & Academic Rigor First combined with real-world systems shipping. Unlike typical student branch template sites, IEEE CS MBITS operates as a crucible where student engineers publish to IEEE Xplore, build low-latency kernel allocators and FPGA testbenches, and compete at the highest tier of statewide engineering hackathons.

## Product Principles
1. **Uncompromising Systems Craft:** We prioritize production code, silicon hardware, and publishable research over generic coursework.
2. **Accessible Editorial Luxury:** Fluid inertial scrolling, high-contrast typography, and tactile feedback without distracting visual noise or unprompted audio.
3. **Transparent Operational Cadence:** Real schedules, genuine archive records, verified leadership sheets, and immediate membership ingest.
